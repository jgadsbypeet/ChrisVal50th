How Do I Get There?

Step-by-Step Directions

Address: Curlew Rowing Club, Crane St, London SE10 9DG

  1. Step 1: Use public transport where possible. Parking is limited and very expensive, so this is the best travel option.
  2. Step 2: Travel to Cutty Sark DLR station, then walk toward Greenwich town centre and follow signs for the riverside. It is approximately a 10-minute walk.
  3. Step 3: You can also travel to Greenwich station via London Bridge (National Rail/Overground route), then walk through Greenwich town centre toward the riverside and Crane Street.
  4. Step 4: If coming via North Greenwich Tube, use Crane St via Romney Rd and Park Row.

Public transport is strongly recommended because local parking is very limited and very expensive.
